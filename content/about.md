I'm an undergraduate in Translation and Interpreting Studies at Boğaziçi University in Istanbul. My research sits between translation and NLP: how machine translation systems are evaluated, where they go wrong — gender bias in Turkish→English translation, for example — and how multilingual models handle languages other than English.

I spent an Erasmus exchange at Universitat Rovira i Virgili in Tarragona, Spain.
